# ⚡ Vector 2035: Hydro-Voltaic Synergy (Direct Electron Generation)

In the 2035 iteration, the GASC system evolves from a purely mechanical turbine into a **Hybrid-Electro-Kinetic Transducer**. By utilizing functionalized 2D-materials, we harvest electrical energy directly from the fluid-solid interface.

## 1. The Streaming Potential Effect ($\Phi_s$)
When high-velocity water ($v > 20\,\text{m/s}$) flows over a charged surface (Graphene-Oxide coated Tesla discs), an electrical potential is generated due to the displacement of the **Electric Double Layer (EDL)**.

**The Helmholtz-Smoluchowski Relation:**
$$\Phi_s = \frac{\epsilon \cdot \zeta \cdot \Delta P}{\eta_{visc} \cdot \kappa}$$

Where:
*   $\epsilon$: Permittivity of the medium.
*   $\zeta$ (Zeta-Potential): Surface charge of the functionalized discs.
*   $\Delta P$: Hydrodynamic pressure gradient along the disc radius.
*   $\kappa$: Conductivity of the fluid.

## 2. Graphene-Interface Interaction (Vector 2035 Edge)
In 2035, we utilize **Graphene-Nanogratings** on the Tesla discs. This creates a "quantum friction" effect where the kinetic energy of the water molecules is converted directly into a current flow ($I_{gen}$) within the graphene layer.

**Induced Current Density ($J$):**
$$J = \sigma_{cond} \cdot (E + v \times B_{int})$$

*Innovation:* We no longer rely solely on the torque of the shaft. The discs themselves act as the **active anode/cathode**, harvesting electrons as the water "shaves" over the surface.

## 3. Total Energy Output ($P_{total, 2035}$)
The system now generates energy from two distinct physical sources:
$$P_{total} = P_{mechanical} + P_{hydrovoltaic}$$

$$P_{total} = (\tau \cdot \omega) + \int_{A} (J \cdot E) \, dA$$

*   $\tau \cdot \omega$: Mechanical torque (Tesla principle).
*   $J \cdot E$: Direct electrical harvest (Hydro-voltaic principle).
