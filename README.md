## 🌊 Vision: Selective Momentum Shearing
The **GASC-Framework** represents a radical deconstruction of classical hydropower. Instead of relying on static head pressure and invasive dam infrastructure, it focuses on the **Selective Shearing** of the turbulent boundary layer in high-velocity vertical flows. 

The architecture aims for a modular, maintenance-free kinetic energy harvesting system that allows the core of a water column to accelerate continuously while "shaving" energy from its periphery.

1.  **Selective Annular Shearing:** Harvesting kinetic energy via the **Shear Ratio ($\sigma$)** without interrupting core acceleration.
