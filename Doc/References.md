# 📚 Scientific References & Theoretical Baselines

To ensure the validity of the **GASC-Framework**, the following established physical principles and empirical studies are utilized as foundational benchmarks.

## 1. Boundary Layer Theory (Prandtl-Schlichting)
The calculation of $\delta(x)$ for turbulent flows in high-velocity conduits follows the semi-empirical relations established by **L. Prandtl** and **H. Schlichting**.
*   *Reference:* Schlichting, H., & Gersten, K. (2016). *Boundary-Layer Theory*. Springer Berlin Heidelberg.
*   *Application:* Basis for the **Shear Ratio ($\sigma$)** and the positioning of the HSB-unit.

## 2. Tesla-Turbine & Viscous Flow (Adhesion Principles)
While Nicola Tesla’s original 1913 patent claims high efficiency, modern experimental data for small-scale disc-runners provide the realistic $\eta_{visc} \approx 0.75$ baseline used in GASC-V1.2.
*   *Reference:* Rice, W. (1991). *Tesla Turbines*. In "Turbomachinery International".
*   *Application:* Efficiency parameters for the **Tesla-Toroid Turbine (TTT)** modules.

## 3. Pump-as-Turbine (PAT) & PRV Comparison
The benchmarking against PAT systems in municipal water networks (PRV stations) is based on standard hydraulic recovery research.
*   *Reference:* Ramos, H. M., et al. (2010). *Energy recovery in water supply systems: Pump as Turbine (PAT) selection*. Journal of Energy Policy.
*   *Application:* Contrast analysis for **GASC as a non-invasive, modular alternative** to fixed-blade PAT systems.

## 4. Coandă Effect & Flow Attachment
The re-injection stability via the **Vector-Injection-Return (VIR)** utilizes the principles of fluid attachment to curved surfaces.
*   *Reference:* Panitz, T., & Wark, C. E. (1963). *The Coanda Effect*. Journal of Fluid Mechanics.
*   *Application:* Justification for the **Injection Angle ($\alpha < 5^\circ$)** and $\Delta P_c$ calculations.
