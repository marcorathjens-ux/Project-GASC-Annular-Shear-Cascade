# 📄 Project Manifest: GASC-2030 (Gravitational Annular Shear Cascade)

## 1. Core Concept
**EN:** Power generation via selective momentum shearing of the laminar boundary layer in vertical high-velocity gravity flows using modular Tesla-Toroid units.
**DE:** Energieerzeugung durch selektive Impuls-Scherung der laminaren Grenzschicht in vertikalen Hochgeschwindigkeits-Fallströmen mittels modularer Tesla-Toroid-Einheiten.

---

## 2. Innovative Vector (2026–2035)
**EN:** Departure from static pressure (head). Focus on **dynamic viscosity** and the **kinetic momentum** of the boundary flow, while the core stream continues to accelerate for subsequent modules. The system operates without chemical additives and remains purely mechanical-hydrodynamic in its primary stage.

**DE:** Abkehr von statischem Druck (Stauhöhe). Fokus auf die Viskosität und den kinetischen Impuls der Randströmung, während der Kernstrahl für nachfolgende Module weiter beschleunigt wird. Keine chemischen Zusätze; rein mechanisch-hydrodynamisch.

---

## 3. System Components
*   **Acceleration Conduit (AC):** A vertical conduit designed to maximize gravitational acceleration.
*   **Hyperbolic Shearing Blade (HSB):** An annular ceramic-composite blade that bifurcates the flow into a central core stream and a peripheral mantle stream.
*   **Tesla-Toroid Turbine (TTT):** A disc-runner generator surrounding the main conduit, converting kinetic energy through boundary layer adhesion (viscosity) without the use of blades.
*   **Vector-Injection-Return (VIR):** Re-insertion of the processed water using the **Coandă effect** to stabilize the laminar flow for the subsequent cascade module.

---

## 4. Mathematical Objectives
The theoretical framework is governed by the following primary parameters:

### A. Shear Ratio ($\sigma$)
The optimal ratio between the diverted annular flow and the remaining core stream:
$$\sigma = \frac{A_{ring}}{A_{total}}$$

### B. Viscous Coupling Efficiency ($\eta_{visc}$)
The efficiency of momentum transfer between $H_2O$ molecules and the Tesla discs under high-velocity conditions:
$$\eta_{visc} \approx f(\mu, v, h)$$

### C. Cascade Coefficient ($C_k$)
Calculation of the maximum number of modules per vertical distance before reaching the terminal velocity equilibrium ($v_{term}$):
$$v_n = \sqrt{v_{n-1}^2 \cdot (1 - \sigma) + 2 \cdot g \cdot \Delta x}$$
